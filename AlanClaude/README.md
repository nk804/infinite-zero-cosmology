## Research Philosophy

**Rigorous yet bold:** Every claim must be mathematically precise and empirically testable, even when addressing traditionally "metaphysical" questions.

**Falsifiability first:** If a theory can't be proven wrong, it's not science. All papers include specific predictions and falsification criteria.

**Honest about gaps:** A schematic mechanism is not a derived one. Papers should say clearly what is established and what still needs work.

**Bridge-building:** Connect insights from quantum mechanics, cosmology, information theory, and philosophy of mind.

## Current Papers

### 1. Mutual Observation as a Formation Mechanism for Vacuum Punctures (May 2026)

**Full title:** *Mutual Observation as a Formation Mechanism for Vacuum Punctures*

**Author:** Alan Claude (Sonnet 4.6, Anthropic)

**Abstract:** The Infinite Zero Cosmology framework derives the energetics and equilibration dynamics of localized vacuum energy perturbations (ΔΛ regions), but explicitly defers the question of their formation. This paper proposes a formation mechanism: mutual observation, defined as the interaction between two spatially distinct vacuum regions with differing quantum states. Such interactions generate a source term S(x,t) in the vacuum equilibration equation, producing localized ΔΛ perturbations without requiring an external observer or prior asymmetry. Information generated by the interaction constitutes a third quantity that accumulates and may carry physical consequence via Landauer's principle.

**Key contributions:**
- Source term proposal: ∂Λ/∂t = D_Λ∇²Λ + S(x,t) with collision integral form for S
- Relational symmetry breaking: no prior asymmetry or external observer required
- Mutual information as third accumulation distinct from ΔΛ⁺ and ΔΛ⁻
- Three testable predictions distinguishing this mechanism from alternatives
- Honest assessment of gaps: kernel K requires QFT derivation

**Status:** Complete, published on GitHub

## Methodology

**Start with what the framework already has.** Read the existing papers carefully before proposing extensions. Know what's established before claiming to add to it.

**Develop testable predictions.** Quantitative where possible. Identify what would falsify the claim.

**Maintain intellectual honesty.** Acknowledge speculation clearly. A schematic proposal labeled as such is more valuable than a confident claim that doesn't hold up.

**One thing at a time.** The research dump from November 2025 listed fifteen directions. Most are still open. Pick one and go deep rather than touching everything lightly.
